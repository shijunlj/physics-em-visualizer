📐 对称性与径向分量抵消原理：
\(\mathrm{d}\vec{A}(\pm\varphi) = \frac{\mu_0 I a\,\mathrm{d}\varphi}{4\pi r'}(\mp\sin\varphi\,\hat{e}_r + \cos\varphi\,\hat{e}_\varphi)\)
径向分量 \(\mathrm{d}A_{1r} + \mathrm{d}A_{2r} = 0\) 抵消，故 \(\vec{A}\) 沿 \(\hat{e}_\varphi\) 方向：
\(\vec{A}(r,z) = \hat{e}_\varphi \frac{\mu_0 I a}{2\pi}\int_0^\pi \frac{\cos\varphi\,\mathrm{d}\varphi}{\sqrt{R^2 + a^2 - 2ar\cos\varphi}}\)
